## About
StickerAI (stickersit.com) is a free, browser-based AI sticker maker that generates custom stickers for WhatsApp, Telegram, iMessage, and Discord. Users type a text prompt describing their sticker idea, choose from 8 artistic styles (Cute Kawaii, Chibi, Pixel Art, Cartoon, Hand-drawn, 3D Rendered, Minimalist, Retro/Vintage), and download generated stickers as PNG files. The tool uses AI-powered image generation and requires no sign-up, no app download, and no payment. It works entirely in the web browser on any device.

## Capabilities
- Text-to-sticker AI generation
- 8 artistic styles: Cute Kawaii, Chibi, Pixel Art, Cartoon, Hand-drawn, 3D Rendered, Minimalist, Retro/Vintage
- Export as PNG (512x512)
- Works on any device with a web browser
- No sign-up required
